Fold stack

Folds run top to bottom, then feed the renderer. Order matters — reordering the same folds makes a different mirror.

Renderer

Glass

Two-colour symmetry: mirrors flip colour as well as position. Any reflection (Polar fold, Mirror line, Frieze, Wallpaper, Pleat) feeds this; the Counterchange fold adds an explicit stripe/checker parity.

Motion

Drift falls through the reflections, spin rotates everything, wobble animates sine-warp folds & ripple.

Record

Record captures the live canvas in realtime. HQ Video renders offline frame-by-frame via WebCodecs — loops close exactly, and Feedback history is rebuilt at export resolution. HQ Still lives up in the Image group.

Chance

Randomize invents a fold stack. Reseed rerolls Shatter patterns.
