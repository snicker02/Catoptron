Glass

Two-colour symmetry: a mirror flips colour as well as position. The Counterchange fold recolours on its own via its Recolor knob — add it and it just works. This dropdown applies the same to reflecting folds (Polar, Mirror line, Wallpaper, Frieze, Pleat) and, when set, overrides a fold’s own Recolor.
Post grade: Exposure/Contrast/Saturation/Warmth colour-grade the final image; Posterize (0 = off) bands it; Scanlines adds a CRT sweep. All apply to every renderer including Feedback.
Signal glitch: channel split/swap corrupt RGB, block dropout punches holes, dither crushes to a palette, signal noise adds static, interlace combs scanlines. Reseed rerolls the block patterns.

Fold stack

Folds run top to bottom, then feed the renderer. Order matters — reordering the same folds makes a different mirror.

IFS — self-similar fold recursion

Wraps the whole fold stack in a self-similar loop: each pass runs your folds, then contracts toward the fixed point (with optional rotation), turning the stack into a fractal generator. More iterations = deeper recursion; lower contraction = tighter nesting. Z-tunnel adds perspective so the recursion tunnels into (+) or bulges out of (−) the screen — a 3D Droste. Off = a single normal pass.

Fluid — velocity + dye sim

A real fluid solver running in the background: velocity is advected, stirred, made incompressible by a pressure solve, and carries a dye field that the source image is injected into. Dye mix blends that flowing dye over your render (1 = pure fluid). Add the Fluid warp fold to push the fold stack around by the same velocity field instead. Dye source picks what gets injected: the current render (so the fluid carries your whole workspace — folds, IFS, colour) or the raw source image. Hold the left mouse button and drag on the canvas to stir it. Higher Pressure = more incompressible (and slower); Vorticity re-injects swirl the solver damps out.

Motion

Drift falls through the reflections, spin rotates everything, wobble undulates the whole pattern. Pulse breathes the zoom, sway drifts the framing, hue cycle rotates colours over time. Stutter quantises the clock into steps, jitter shakes the framing per frame, and glitch burst spikes channel-split/noise/dropout on a beat (0 = still). Motion blur smears moving areas by blending recent frames (static areas stay sharp) — it blurs all motion, including audio-driven moves.

Catoptron — User Guide

Catoptron turns a photo (or a built-in pattern) into kaleidoscopic, fractal, and conformal art. You build an effect by stacking coordinate “folds” and choosing a renderer that reflects and recurses the result, then dress it with color, framing, and motion. Everything runs live on the GPU — nothing is uploaded.

The idea in one picture

SOURCE          FOLD STACK              RENDERER          GLASS           MOTION
image   →   fold₁ → fold₂ → …  →   Panes/Droste/… →   color/frame →   animate

Read it left to right. Each fold bends the coordinate grid; folds compose in order, so the same folds in a different order give a different result. The renderer mirrors/repeats that bent grid into the final structure. Glass handles color and framing. Motion animates it for video.

Best habit: add one fold, see what it does, then add the next. Stacks of 2–4 folds already go a long way.

Quick start

  • Source: Load image for your own photo, or pick a built-in pattern (orbs, plasma, rings, stripes, waves, voronoi, truchet, halftone, or a grid/polar/checker probe) and shape it with the Scale / Hue / Variation knobs to learn an effect against a clean reference.
  • Renderer: start on Panes.
  • Fold stack: pick an operator in the add dropdown and click +. Try Polar fold first — instant kaleidoscope.
  • Drag its sliders; add a second fold (Swirl or Spiral).
  • Open Presets to see full recipes. Export PNG when happy, or Record / HQ Video for animation.

Shared controls: Depth (reflection/recursion steps), Step scale (zoom per level), Twist (rotation per level), Shift X/Y & Zoom (pan/zoom), mirrored (flip alternating tiles), and Feedback amount + Mosh (datamosh block-displacement of the fed-back frame) — both Feedback renderer only. Feedback also has a Reaction-diffusion mode — Gray-Scott Turing patterns grown from the image, where Pull/Rotate become Feed/Kill (pattern type) and Feedback becomes Image drive (how strongly the photo shapes the pattern: 0 = free patterns, 1 = photo dominates).

The fold stack

  • Add from the dropdown; it appends to the bottom.
  • Reorder with ↑/↓ — order matters (Swirl-then-Mirror ≠ Mirror-then-Swirl).
  • Remove with ×.
  • Reset to defaults (button at the bottom of each fold) returns that fold’s parameters, origin and angle to their factory defaults — undoable.
  • Numeric params are sliders; choice params are dropdowns (mode, style, symmetry group…).
  • Mode-aware: multi-mode folds show only the current mode’s parameters.
  • Origin (⊕): click it and drag on the canvas to place that fold’s center — great for off-center swirls and lenses.

The big multi-mode banks

  • Complex — up to four complex functions in sequence (1/z, z², sqrt, exp, log, the trig/hyperbolic families and their inverses), with per-axis frequency and optional mirror.
  • Complex sum — instead of chaining, add functions with individual weights: a pre-conditioner tier (reciprocal, log, sqrt, exp) plus a 15-function sum tier, with an input affine.
  • Wave bank — one fold, 17 wave styles (waves22, dc_gnarly’s 8 nested-trig modes, vibration2, waves2b Jacobi/Bessel, waves_julia/spiral/noise/mobius/power/fisheye/swirl…). Pick Style; only that style’s knobs show. Weight blends; Seed feeds input back.
  • Lazy — the classic lazy-susan family: Susan (circular swirl, movable center via x/y), Travis (square swirl), Jess (n-gon swirl), plus a Sensen post-fold. For Travis/Jess try Amount ≈ 0.5–0.6 to stay in frame.
  • Loonie — circle-inversion bubbles: Loonie (circle), Loonie2 (n-gon/star/circle blend), Loonie3 (parabolic). Radius sizes the bubble independently of Amount; Rotate spins the Loonie2 polygon.
  • Lens bank — the optical zoo in one fold, 12 modes: Brown-Conrady, Mustache, Division, four Fisheye projections (stereographic/equisolid/equidistant/orthographic), Tangential, Petzval swirl, Panini, Anamorphic, Tilt-shift. Pick Mode; only that mode’s knobs show.
  • Shape warp — a warp bank in one fold: pick a Shape (square, circle, diamond, triangle, pentagon, hexagon, flower, star, cloud…) whose boundary drives an inner→outer falloff, then a Warp mode (rotate, scale-radial, swirl, scale-XY, fisheye, shear). Shape-specific knobs appear only for the relevant shape.
  • Tessellated — a tiling/distortion bank: pick a Mode (square power/sine/radial, hex offset, true hex, radial tiling, exp-log, tangent, polynomial, Julia) and a Symmetry (none, X/Y-reflect, quadrant, D4, Dn). Scale sets cell density, Edge blend softens seams, and Grid/Cell rotate with a Rotate pattern spin whole cells. Mode-specific knobs show only when relevant.
  • BusyBrad — the grid version of the lazy family: tiles the plane (Grid size), each cell a Susan/Jess/Combined swirl, with Spin/Twist/Space/N and a Sensen fold. Grid size 0 = a single centered swirl.

Recolor

Counterchange is the one fold that changes color, not geometry: it splits the plane into stripes/checker/pinwheel/rings and recolors alternating regions (negate, hue 180°, desaturate, tint). Use Cell for scale, Angle° for orientation. (The Glass panel has a second, global counterchange.)

Glass — color & framing

  • Frame / Frame width — border vignette-frame.
  • Tint — color + blend amount.
  • Counterchange (global) — off / negate / hue 180° / desaturate / tint, with a fold scale.
  • Chroma — chromatic-aberration fringing. Ripple — final rippling pass. Vignette — darken edges. Grain — film grain. Hue / depth — shift hue with recursion depth.
  • Post grade (every renderer): Exposure, Contrast, Saturation, Warmth, Posterize (0 = off), Scanlines (CRT sweep).
  • Signal glitch: Channel split / Channel swap corrupt RGB, Block dropout punches holes, Dither crushes to a palette, Signal noise adds static, Interlace combs scanlines. Block patterns reroll with Reseed.

Motion — animation

  • Drift slowly evolves fold parameters. Spin rotates the whole view. Rotate is a fixed (animatable) rotation. Wobble gently undulates the whole pattern over time — it animates every fold, plus the Wave folds and Ripple.
  • Pulse breathes the zoom, Sway drifts the framing, Hue cycle rotates all colours over time.
  • Motion glitch: Stutter quantizes the clock into hold-then-jump steps, Jitter shakes framing/rotation per frame, and Glitch burst spikes channel-split/noise/dropout on a beat (fires even with the glass-glitch sliders at 0).
  • Loops are built to close seamlessly when exported as HQ Video.

Presets, export & recording

  • Presets: load a factory recipe, save your own, Randomize, Reseed, and Copy/Paste or Import/Export as text to share.
  • Export PNG at 1×/2×/4× the live view or a fixed height (1080p–2880p) — supersampled (2× SSAA) and downsampled for clean edges when it fits under the GPU limit; Feedback is rebuilt at export resolution.
  • Record (WebM) captures the live canvas; HQ Video renders offline frame-by-frame (loops close exactly, Feedback rebuilt at full res).
  • Aspect locks the composition to any common ratio before export.

Recipes to try

  • Kaleidoscope portrait: Polar fold (6–12) → Swirl (small) → Panes + Tint.
  • Infinite vortex: Spiral → Droste, raise Step scale & Twist.
  • Stained glass: Wallpaper (p6m) → Counterchange (checker, tint) → Panes.
  • Liquid chrome: Wave bank (dc_gnarly) → Feedback, add Chroma + a little Drift.
  • Bubble field: Loonie (Loonie2, sides 6, some Star) → Spherical → Room.
  • Spiral galaxy: Log spiral → Wave bank (waves_spiral) → Droste, Vignette up.
Muddy? Remove the last fold — over-stacking is the usual culprit. Flat? Switch the renderer before adding more folds.
